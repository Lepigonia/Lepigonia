*Two Years in Norway – Nature, Change, and Looking Ahead*

A little over two years ago, I packed up my life and moved to Norway. Fresh out of my biology studies, it felt like the beginning of a completely new chapter. A new country, a new environment, and a new life waiting to be built.
Originally, this journey was not meant to be taken alone. My partner and I made the decision together, leaving familiar ground behind in search of something different. A place where we could build a life, explore new opportunities, and experience the world in a different way.
Norway quickly became much more than just a place to work. For me, it became something of an experiment: what does it actually mean to integrate into a new society? Learning the language, understanding the culture, figuring out the unwritten rules of everyday life — and slowly finding a place within it all.

*Finding a Place in a New Culture*

Integration sounds like a big word, but in reality it is made up of many small moments.
It means struggling through conversations in a language that at first feels unfamiliar. Learning local habits and traditions. Understanding how people interact with each other. And sometimes simply accepting that things work differently than what you are used to.
Over time, Norway stopped feeling like “the new place” and slowly became everyday life. Work, friendships, shared activities — step by step I built a life here.

*The Gift of Nature*

One of the things that has always made Norway special for me is its connection to nature.
The landscapes here are vast, raw, and incredibly free. It doesn’t take much planning to head outside. You can go fishing in a quiet lake, wander through forests looking for mushrooms, or simply pitch a tent somewhere in the wild and spend the night under the open sky.
This sense of freedom is something that has shaped my time here deeply. Many Norwegians live very close to nature, and outdoor life is simply part of everyday culture. For me, that has always been one of the greatest gifts of living here.




*When Plans Change*

As mentioned, I didn’t come here alone.
About a year ago, that part of the story came to a tragic and unexpected end. Losing the person you once planned a future with changes many things. Suddenly, the place that was meant to be shared becomes a space for reflection, rebuilding, and figuring out what comes next.
Moments like that often force you to rethink direction and purpose.





*Looking Toward New Horizons*
Over the past months, a new idea has slowly taken shape.
For quite some time I’ve had the idea of exploring further — not just visiting places, but really traveling. Slowly, independently, and with enough time to experience the world along the way.
The plan that is currently forming is a road trip through Africa. My companion for that adventure will be my old Volkswagen T4, which will hopefully carry me across continents, deserts, mountains, and borders.
Right now, I’m still in the preparation phase. Planning routes, sorting out equipment, dealing with paperwork, and slowly turning a vague idea into something real.

*What This Blog Is About*
This blog is meant to document that journey.
But every journey has a beginning, and mine starts here — in Norway. With the experiences of the last two years, the people I met, the landscapes I explored, and the changes that shaped this time.
From here, the road continues.
*New places. New stories. And hopefully many kilometers ahead.*
